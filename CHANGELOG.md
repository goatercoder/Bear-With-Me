# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — 2026-09-20
- Complete redesign: no arena, timer, XP or stats. Just Oski.
- New 24×24 8-bit Oski (Cal blue-and-gold sweater) with five cumulative injury stages and a dead state.
- Health drains while on distracting sites (30 min to death) or idle (2 h); heals while working (90 min).
- Tiny corner window (toolbar click) plus an overlay of Oski on every web page.
- Notifications at 50%, 20% and death; badge shows health.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-20
- Toolbar icon opens a small window (arena + timer + Start) parked in the top-right corner; clicking again focuses it.
- Stats, quests, pets and settings moved into a drawer behind the ⚙ button.
- Osci evolves through five forms: Cub (Lv 1), Bear (Lv 5), Scholar (Lv 10), Knight (Lv 18), Cosmic (Lv 28).
- Version shown in ⚙ → Settings.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-19
- First release: Pomodoro timer, arena, XP/levels, HP decay and death, streak shields, daily quests,
  battle reports, distraction shield, big arena tab, eight pets.
