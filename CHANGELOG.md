# Changelog

## 2.3.0 — 2026-09-20
- New option: show Oski on web pages **only when you're on a taboo site**. He stays hidden everywhere else.
- Default taboo sites are now clashroyaleapi.com, reddit.com, youtube.com and instagram.com.
- Upgrades keep your pet: health, deaths and a site list you edited all survive. An untouched list moves to
  the new defaults.
- The page overlay now shares the real host-matching code instead of a copy, and decides per page rather
  than per browser.

## 2.2.0 — 2026-09-20
- The Stanford Tree is now a pine: three tiers of boughs, a trunk, and the same angry face.
- Drag Oski anywhere on a web page; the spot is remembered and followed by every tab.
- Drag Oski inside his own window to move that window around the screen; the spot is remembered.
- Fixed a missing saved position snapping him flush into the corner instead of the default spot.

## 2.1.0 — 2026-09-20
- 📌 always-on-top mode: Oski moves into a floating picture-in-picture window above every other window.
- Instant 8% blood hit the moment you land on a taboo site; 20 minutes there now kills him.
- The Stanford Tree appears and beats Oski while you're on a taboo site (blood bursts on every hit).
- Injuries reordered: blood pours from his eyes first, skull exposed near the end.
- Oski is injected into already-open tabs on install/reload, so he shows on every page right away.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-09-20
- Complete redesign: no arena, timer, XP or stats. Just Oski.
- New 24×24 8-bit Oski (Cal blue-and-gold sweater) with five cumulative injury stages and a dead state.
- Health drains while on distracting sites (30 min to death) or idle (2 h); heals while working (90 min).
- Tiny corner window (toolbar click) plus an overlay of Oski on every web page.
- Notifications at 50%, 20% and death; badge shows health.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-20
- Toolbar icon opens a small window (arena + timer + Start) parked in the top-right corner; clicking again focuses it.
- Stats, quests, pets and settings moved into a drawer behind the ⚙ button.
- Osci evolves through five forms: Cub (Lv 1), Bear (Lv 5), Scholar (Lv 10), Knight (Lv 18), Cosmic (Lv 28).
- Version shown in ⚙ → Settings.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-19
- First release: Pomodoro timer, arena, XP/levels, HP decay and death, streak shields, daily quests,
  battle reports, distraction shield, big arena tab, eight pets.
